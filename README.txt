ResizeSnap website bundle

Files:
- index.html: main image resizer (JPG, PNG, WebP)
- about.html, privacy.html, terms.html, contact.html: supporting pages
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml: replace YOUR-DOMAIN.example with the final live domain

Before publishing:
1. Choose a final site name/domain and replace canonical URL, sitemap and robots placeholders.
2. Replace the contact email in contact.html with a real support email.
3. Review privacy/terms to match your actual hosting, analytics, ad and consent setup.
4. Upload the contents of this folder to a static host such as GitHub Pages or Netlify.
5. Test upload, width/height updates, aspect-ratio lock, JPG/PNG/WebP output and downloads on mobile and desktop.
6. Apply to AdSense only after the site has useful original content, working navigation, accurate policy pages and complies with Google's current publisher policies.
7. Only after AdSense approval, add your real publisher ID and ad unit IDs to the marked ad placements in index.html. Placeholder ad boxes are not live ads.

No backend is required. Image processing happens in the visitor's browser.
