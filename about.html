About ResizeSnap

ResizeSnap is a simple online utility designed to help people resize JPG, PNG and WebP images. The tool lets visitors adjust image dimensions, preserve aspect ratio, preview the result and download a resized copy.

Image processing is performed in the visitor’s browser. The selected image is not uploaded to a ResizeSnap server by the image-resizing tool.

For questions or feedback, use our Contact page.
