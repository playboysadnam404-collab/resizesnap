How to resize an image

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device.
  2. Enter a new width or height in pixels.
  3. Keep aspect ratio enabled to avoid stretching.
  4. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP and download your image.

Private and easy to use

Image resizing happens locally in your browser using the canvas feature. Your selected image is not sent to our server. For best results, use a modern browser and avoid extremely large images on older devices.
